Amano help you can actually trust.

Amano is an idea for a home services marketplace in Morocco, built to replace word of mouth with real verification, so finding a plumber or electrician stops being a gamble.

See the idea
The problem

Right now, trust is the missing piece.

In Morocco today, finding someone to fix a leak or rewire a socket usually means asking a neighbour, scrolling a WhatsApp group, or calling whoever answers first. There is no way to check who actually did good work, what it should cost, or who is responsible if something goes wrong.

No verification

Anyone can claim to be a professional. There is no consistent way to check.

No record

A great local craftsman's reputation lives in conversations, not anywhere you can search.

No transparency

Pricing is negotiated case by case, with no reference point beforehand.

No accountability

If a job goes badly, there is rarely a clear way to make it right.

The idea

A place where reputation is real, not word of mouth.

Amano's plan is to give every professional a verified profile built on real work: identity checks, past jobs, honest reviews, and clear pricing, so a homeowner can choose with confidence instead of guessing.

Why it matters

Trust is the product, not a feature.

Plenty of platforms help people find a phone number. Amano's focus is narrower and harder: making sure that once you find someone, you can actually trust them, and that good professionals get credit for the work they've already done.

Verification first. A profile means something because it was checked, not just typed in.

Reputation that travels. A professional's history builds up in one place instead of staying scattered across word of mouth.

Clear expectations. Both sides know the price, the schedule, and what happens if something goes wrong, before the work starts.

From the founder

Why I'm building this

Before studying software engineering, I worked more than ten different manual and service jobs, and got genuinely good at a couple of them. I know what it feels like to do solid work and still stay invisible, because your next customer only finds you if someone happens to mention your name.

Amano started from that experience, not from a market report. I'm still testing the idea, talking to real customers and professionals, before building anything at scale. This page is part of that process.

Zakaria El Mountassir
